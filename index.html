What's on · September 2026

A few events and markets that may be worth planning into your stay this month.

4 – 8 September · Fuengirola (Los Boliches)
Feria de Los Boliches

The vibrant neighbourhood fair of Los Boliches, with traditional daytime celebrations, live music, dancing, casetas, and seaside festivities.

8 – 14 September · Álora
Romería de la Virgen de Flores

Álora celebrates its patron saint with a traditional pilgrimage to the sanctuary, horse parades, music, and a festive outdoor atmosphere.

5 – 10 September · Mijas Pueblo
Feria de Mijas Pueblo

The annual village fair honouring the Virgen de la Peña, with processions, flamenco, live music, and local food.

11 – 12 September · Guaro
Luna Mora

An enchanting festival lighting up the historic white village after dark with thousands of candles, Andalusian-Sufi music, and street markets.

18 – 21 September · Torrox
Torrox Oktoberfest

A lively coastal celebration with traditional German beer, hearty food, music, and a festive beer-tent atmosphere.

20 September · Torremolinos
San Miguel pilgrimage (Romería)

A grand traditional pilgrimage with Andalusian dress, horses and decorated carts heading to the pine forests of Los Manantiales.

25 – 29 September · Torremolinos
Feria de San Miguel

Torremolinos' major annual town fair, with daytime feria culture, evening casetas, concerts, funfair attractions and dancing.

Information and house rules

Welcome to Finca Flamenco Holiday Suites! These house rules help ensure peace, safety and comfort for all our guests. Thank you in advance for your cooperation — we wish you a wonderful stay!

Check-in and check-out

Check-in: 14:00 – 23:00 on the day of arrival.
Check-out: before 11:00.
Arrival after 23:00 or before 11:00 must be confirmed at least 24 hours in advance by email or WhatsApp. Late check-out is possible if there's no check-in that day for the suite, for €5 per hour per suite.

Age policy

Only guests aged 18 and over may stay with us, with a valid ID or passport. Exceptions are possible for family members or friends of the owners.

Noise and quiet hours

Please respect quiet hours between 00:00 and 08:00. Commercial events or loud parties are not allowed without written permission.

Swimming pool

From sunrise to sunset.

Smoking

Allowed on the patio and at the outdoor bar. At the pool, only at the designated smoking table. Not allowed in the rooms — your own balcony is fine.

Outdoor bar and kitchen

The trust bar works on a self-service basis: take your drink and honestly note what you've had. Settle up when you check out. The outdoor kitchen (with utensils, plates and cutlery) is free to use — please don't bring these items to your room.

Own food and drinks

Not permitted on the patio, at the outdoor bar, or at the pool — please use the trust bar in these shared spaces. In your own room or on your balcony, it's of course fine.

Barbecue and open fires

Open fires or your own barbecues are not permitted. The outdoor kitchen and its BBQ are free to use.

Pets

Not allowed, except for certified service animals.

Visitors & photography

Overnight visitors are only allowed with prior approval. Commercial photography or filming is not permitted without written consent.

Departure

Please leave the room as you found it: take your belongings, leave no trash, wipe down tables and surfaces, wash and put away any dishes used, and leave the bathroom tidy. Return your keys at check-out — lost keys will result in the cylinders being replaced at the guest's expense.

Damage

Guests are liable for damage caused by themselves or their companions. Please report damage immediately via WhatsApp or email. Compensation is due within 7 days of departure, based on replacement value.

Breakfast

Daily: 09:00 – 10:30.
Served in the breakfast room — you're welcome to enjoy it on the patio or in your room instead.

Linens, towels & laundry

For longer stays, towels and bed linen are refreshed on request. We don't enter your room by default during your stay, to respect your privacy. Two washing machines are available in the storage room by the patio: €3.50 per cycle (40/60°C, max 7kg).

Complaints

Aircon & water

Air conditioners aren't freezers

Our air conditioners are brand new and cool the room noticeably within 5 minutes. A few friendly guidelines:

  • Only run it while you're actually in the room — heading to the pool or out for the day? Please turn it off.
  • Keep windows and doors closed for the best cooling and least waste.
  • Set it to 22–23°C. Lower (17–18°C) won't cool the room faster, but can make it uncomfortably cold, waste energy, and in some cases even cause the unit to freeze up and stop cooling altogether.

Why in the bedroom?

The units are placed in the bedrooms on purpose, for a comfortable night's sleep. A bedroom is a small space — full power isn't needed and can quickly lead to a sore throat or "AC headache." Better: steady, gentle cooling.

Water

In southern Spain, water is a precious resource, especially in summer during droughts. The municipality then applies water-saving measures, up to and including full shutdowns every other day or fluctuating water pressure. We keep at least 4,000 litres of water in reserve in case supply is interrupted.

This can mean reduced water pressure in your room or bathroom. Please use water mindfully: turn off the tap while brushing your teeth, shaving, or lathering up, and take shorter showers.
